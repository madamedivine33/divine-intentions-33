Why I Create Honey Jars

I do not create honey jars to bind, control, or override another person's will. I believe deeply in free will, and my work will never cross that line. A honey jar, as I conjure it, is an act of devotion to you — a working that sweetens your own energy, deepens your relationship with yourself, and opens the path for what is aligned to find you. The more fully you love and honor yourself, the more you heal, and the more that healing ripples into every relationship you carry — romantic, familial, and beyond. This jar is conjured to help you attract and achieve, not to force. What it draws to you is meant to meet a version of you that is already whole.
